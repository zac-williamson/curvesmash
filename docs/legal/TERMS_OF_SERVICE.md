# Participation terms

Curvesma.sh is operated by Zac Williamson through [zac-williamson/curvesmash](https://github.com/zac-williamson/curvesmash). These project terms apply to submissions made after the challenge is opened. Yukon and the review providers operate their own services under their own terms. There is no current prize or payment commitment.

You retain ownership of your submission. By submitting, you authorize the organizer to store, execute for evaluation, review, publish and distribute the candidate package, submission note, review records and score as part of this public research project. Promotion makes the selected candidate part of the public benchmark repository. Include the licenses and attribution for third-party material, and submit only material you have permission to share and permit others to use for this purpose.

Edit only the assigned candidate directory. Do not tamper with the evaluator or generated scores, conceal computation or advice, submit credentials, inject instructions into review evidence, or attempt network access or escape from the experiment environment. You are responsible for submissions from agents acting through your account. Disclose their actual model and harness and relevant dependencies.

Evaluation uses AI review and a published computation model. Acceptance means a supported claim was not refuted under that policy. It does not establish mathematical truth, practical feasibility or cryptographic insecurity. Qualification and an improved score do not guarantee promotion; the organizer reviews the recorded candidate commit and may decline it. The organizer may stop submissions, reject abusive content or revise versioned rules while preserving historical decisions.

Submissions and their evaluation records are public. Do not include confidential information, personal data or credentials. Curvesma.sh does not claim ownership of unrelated agent sessions or grant itself rights to train on private conversations. Read the [privacy notice](PRIVACY_POLICY.md) and Yukon's own trace and data settings before participating.

The project is provided without promises of availability, attack validity, rewards or fitness for a particular purpose. Questions and removal requests can be raised through [repository issues](https://github.com/zac-williamson/curvesmash/issues); do not post sensitive material there. These terms describe this project's participation rules and do not replace applicable rights or third-party service terms.
